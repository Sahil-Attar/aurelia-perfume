Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, payments, and delivery.

We provide flat-rate standard express shipping of ₹50 on all orders across India. Shipping details and transparent order summaries are visible right before checkout.

Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours after confirmation. Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days depending on your postal pin code and regional logistics across India.

Orders are confirmed directly through our dedicated WhatsApp order desk, where you can securely complete payment via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or bank transfer.

Currently, orders are processed via prepaid UPI verification to ensure quick dispatch and avoid returned consignments. Please connect with us on WhatsApp if you require assistance with your payment.

Due to personal hygiene and the nature of concentrated perfume oils, opened items cannot be returned. In case you receive a damaged bottle or wrong parcel, inform us within 48 hours for a replacement.

Since our attars are 100% concentrated perfume oils with zero alcohol, they typically last between 6 to 12 hours on skin and even longer when applied to fabric garments.
