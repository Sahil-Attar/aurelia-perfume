About Scent Aura X

Crafting genuine roll-on perfume oils that express your natural aura. Traditional distillation meets modern wearable fragrance.

THE PHILOSOPHY

Pure Fragrance, Lasting Impression

At Scent Aura X, we believe fragrance should feel intimate and personal — not harsh or overpowering. Traditional commercial sprays are often diluted with up to 80% alcohol, leading to a strong synthetic burst that evaporates in mere minutes.

We formulate pure, concentrated oil attars that blend seamlessly with your skin's natural heat. Rich agarwood, soothing white musk, velvety benzoin amber, and distilled Damascena roses blossom progressively throughout the day.
