CUSTOMER CARE & POLICIES

Shipping Policy

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

At Scent Aura X, we aim to deliver your favourite fragrances safely and conveniently across India.

1. Delivery Locations

We currently offer shipping across India, subject to courier and postal service availability at the customer's location.

2. Shipping Charges

  • A shipping charge of ₹50 per order is currently displayed at checkout.
  • The applicable shipping charge will be shown in your order summary before you confirm your order.
  • Any changes to shipping charges will be communicated before order confirmation.

3. Order Processing

  • Orders are processed after successful order confirmation.
  • Our team verifies the order details and prepares the products for dispatch.
  • Dispatch timelines may vary depending on product availability, order volume, holidays and operational conditions.

4. Estimated Delivery

  • Delivery timelines depend on the destination PIN code and the shipping service provider.
  • An estimated delivery timeline will be communicated during order confirmation or dispatch.
  • Please note that delays may occur due to weather conditions, transportation disruptions, public holidays or other circumstances beyond our control.

5. Order Tracking

  • Where tracking is available, tracking details will be shared with customers after dispatch.
  • Customers can use the provided tracking information to follow their shipment.

6. Incorrect Address or Failed Delivery

  • Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping information, including their name, mobile number, PIN code and delivery address.
  • If an order cannot be delivered due to an incorrect address, unavailable recipient, or repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts, please contact us for assistance.
  • Any additional shipping costs arising from a customer-requested reshipment will be communicated before reshipping.

7. Damaged or Incorrect Deliveries

  • Please inspect your parcel upon delivery.
  • If you receive a damaged, leaking, defective or incorrect product, contact us as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Please share your order details, photographs and an unboxing video if available.
  • We will investigate the issue and arrange an appropriate resolution in accordance with our Returns & Refund Policy and applicable consumer laws.

8. Delivery Delays

  • Although we make every effort to deliver orders within the estimated timeline, we cannot guarantee exact delivery dates.
  • Unexpected delays caused by courier partners, weather conditions, strikes, public holidays or other external circumstances may affect delivery.
  • We will assist customers in tracking and resolving delayed shipments.

9. Contact Us
