CUSTOMER CARE & POLICIES

Returns & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

At Scent Aura X, we carefully pack and inspect every order before dispatch. Customer satisfaction is important to us, and we want you to receive exactly what you ordered.

Due to the personal-use nature of fragrance products, we follow a strict return and refund policy to maintain product hygiene and prevent misuse.

1. Return Eligibility

Returns and refunds are not accepted for:

  • Products that have been opened, used, partially used, or tampered with.
  • Products with broken or removed original seals, where applicable.
  • Products damaged after delivery due to customer handling or misuse.
  • Products returned with missing components, altered packaging, or missing accessories.
  • Products returned with a different item, bottle, fragrance, or quantity from the product originally dispatched.
  • Change of mind, personal fragrance preference, or dissatisfaction with a fragrance's scent or performance after purchase.
Important: Attar and fragrance products are personal-use items. Once opened or used, they cannot be returned for reasons of personal preference.

2. Wrong, Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive:

  • A different product from the one ordered.
  • A damaged or leaking bottle.
  • A defective product.
  • A product that does not match its advertised description.

Please contact us as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of delivery, with your order details, clear photographs and an unboxing video, if available.

After verification, we will arrange an appropriate replacement or refund, as applicable. The return and replacement shipping costs for verified fulfilment errors or product defects will be borne by us.

Your statutory consumer rights are not affected by this policy.

3. Product Verification and Inspection

For any approved return, the returned product must be submitted for inspection.

We reserve the right to reject a return or refund request if our inspection and dispatch records establish that:

  • The returned product is different from the product originally supplied.
  • The product has been used, refilled, altered, or tampered with after delivery.
  • The original product or its essential components are missing.
  • The return does not meet the applicable return conditions.

Sending a different product in place of the original purchased product will not qualify for a refund.

4. Refund Processing

  • Refunds will be initiated only after the reported issue has been verified and the refund has been approved.
  • Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method or another mutually agreed payment method, as applicable.
  • The time taken for the refund to reflect may depend on the payment provider or bank. Approved refunds will be processed within the applicable legal timeframe.

5. Order Cancellation

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted as soon as possible, before the order is dispatched.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. Any applicable statutory rights will remain unaffected.

6. Return Shipping Charges

  • For approved returns arising from a verified wrong, damaged or defective product supplied by us, we will bear the applicable return shipping charges.
  • For any return voluntarily accepted for reasons not attributable to us, the customer will be responsible for the applicable return shipping charges, where permitted.

Please include your order details and supporting photographs or videos so that we can assist you efficiently.
