FRAGRANCE CRAFT

Pure Artisanal Concentrates

Understanding traditional attar oils and how they evolve throughout the day.

Zero Alcohol

100% Pure Perfume Oil

Attars are pure fragrance extracts blended into fine botanical bases without harsh alcohol solvents, providing a rich and intimate scent bubble that stays close to your pulse points.

Natural Evolution

Layered Dry Down

Because there is no flash-evaporating alcohol, pure perfume oils unfurl slowly with your natural body heat, revealing fresh top notes first, followed by soothing heart florals and deep wood bases.

Direct Application

How to Apply

Roll a small drop onto your inner wrists, neck, or collar. Gently tap without rubbing harshly to allow the delicate fragrance molecules to bloom naturally throughout the day.
